How to play

Set your tilt range once in the testing dashboard (front/back/ left/right) — it's saved on the board and shared here automatically. Pick a Control axis (pitch = fore/aft, roll = left/right); the midpoint of the matching limits becomes centre and the game triggers part-way to each limit — easier than holding an exact angle. Tilt toward the + limit to jump (hold for a higher/longer jump), tilt the other way to duck. No saved limits? It falls back to the ±threshold slider. No board? ↑/Space jump, ↓ duck, Enter start.
